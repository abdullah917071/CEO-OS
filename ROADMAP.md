# Roadmap

The authoritative milestone sequence and active acceptance criteria are maintained in `PLANS.md`.

- Foundation and CEO text prototype establish a runnable, modular vertical slice.
- Durability and memory follow before computer, browser, and voice control.
- Multi-agent execution follows only after task cancellation, permissions, evidence, and recovery exist.
- Provider integrations arrive through the capability/integration boundary, starting with MCP and Google services.
- Telephony, restaurant workflows, advertising, communications, and business operating agents build on those foundations.
- Skills, API generation, proactive goals, and production hardening complete the long-range platform.

Roadmap order may change only through a documented decision; safety prerequisites may not be skipped.
