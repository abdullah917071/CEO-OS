# Vision

CEO OS is a personal AI operating company, not merely a voice assistant. The owner communicates primarily with one CEO intelligence. That CEO understands goals, compiles relevant context, plans, delegates to permanent or temporary specialists, applies policy, and reports outcomes supported by evidence.

The system should eventually operate personal and business workflows across memory, files, terminal, macOS applications, browsers, voice, calls, email, calendar, messaging, advertising, analytics, sales, finance, operations, research, software development, reporting, and automation.

## Product principles

- Goals and success conditions are the unit of work; clicks are implementation details.
- Deterministic function, official API, DOM automation, Accessibility API, then visual control is the fallback order.
- Long-term knowledge is external to model context and retrieved deliberately.
- Every meaningful task is observable, resumable, interruptible, and auditable.
- The CEO plans and delegates; tools perform effects; integrations adapt providers.
- Capabilities are discoverable and modular so adding a provider does not rewrite the CEO.
- High autonomy is bounded by risk, budgets, approvals, isolation, and a global stop control.

## Success

The finished system can continuously understand the owner’s businesses and life, coordinate many specialized workers, act through the best available interface, explain what happened, recover from failure, and safely accept entirely new capabilities years later.

V1 must not mimic that whole future. It must establish contracts and a reliable vertical slice that future capabilities can attach to without architectural replacement.
