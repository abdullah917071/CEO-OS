# Communications

Email, calendar, SMS, messaging, and telephony use provider-neutral service contracts. The CEO requests outcomes such as sending an approved message or completing a call objective; adapters translate those requests to provider APIs.

Communication effects are at least R2 and require identity, recipients, content/objective, authority, deduplication key, and evidence of provider acceptance. Drafting is distinct from sending. Incoming content is untrusted and cannot grant permissions.

Telephony separates provider, call manager, audio bridge, live conversation state, transcript, summary, and legal recording policy. Negotiation policies explicitly define targets, maximums, commitments, and allowed concessions.

No real communications provider is implemented in Phase 1.
